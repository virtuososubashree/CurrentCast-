
CurrentCast: A Weather Report for Blackouts

What problem are we solving? Unpredictable power outages disrupt critical infrastructure, industrial operations, and residential life during severe weather events.

The Problem in Detail Utility companies, manufacturing plants, hospitals, and everyday citizens face severe disruptions from sudden blackouts caused by weather anomalies. These unpredicted outages happen frequently during heavy storms or heatwaves, costing industries significant revenue in downtime, endangering critical care, and causing widespread inconvenience because people lack localized, actionable blackout forecasts to prepare backup systems.

Our Solution We built a predictive dashboard that acts as a "weather report for blackouts." It correlates real-time meteorological data with local grid vulnerabilities to forecast the probability, timeline, and location of upcoming power outages, giving users critical advance warning to activate backup energy systems or pause sensitive operations.

Target Users Grid operators, industrial facility managers, healthcare administrators, and residential communities in weather-vulnerable zones.

What makes it distinctive? While standard weather apps only predict rain or wind, CurrentCast translates those meteorological variables directly into grid impact probability. It bridges the gap between weather forecasting and electrical infrastructure management to provide actionable grid intelligence.
